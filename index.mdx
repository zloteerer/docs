## Where to Begin

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your account">
    Sign up at [app.zolt.io](https://app.zolt.io/signup) and verify your email address to activate your account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set up your workspace">
    Configure your workspace name, invite teammates, and assign roles so everyone has the right level of access.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create your first project">
    Add a project, break it down into tasks, and assign work to team members to get your first workflow running.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect your tools">
    Link Zolt to the apps your team already uses — from communication tools to cloud storage — via integrations or the API.
  </Step>
</Steps>
