## Response Time SLAs

Zolt's first response time commitment depends on your plan. Response times are measured from when your message is received during the applicable support hours.

| Plan | First Response |
|------|----------------|
| Free | 3 business days |
| Pro | 1 business day |
| Business | 4 hours |

<Note>
  For urgent production issues on the **Business plan**, include the word **"URGENT"** in your email subject line (e.g., `URGENT: Webhook delivery completely down`). This triggers the priority support queue and ensures your ticket is escalated immediately to a senior support engineer.
</Note>

## Before You Contact Us

Taking a few minutes to check these resources first often resolves the issue faster than waiting for a support reply:

- **Check the FAQ** — Browse the [Frequently Asked Questions](/support/faq) page for answers to the most common questions about accounts, billing, projects, and integrations.
- **Search the documentation** — Use the search bar at the top of this page to find guides, reference articles, and tutorials relevant to your issue.
- **Check the status page** — Visit [status.zolt.io](https://status.zolt.io) to confirm there are no active incidents affecting the feature you're using.
- **Gather your workspace ID** — Find this under **Settings > General**. Including it in your support message allows the team to look up your account without delays.
- **Note your browser and OS** — Record the browser name and version (e.g., Chrome 124) and your operating system (e.g., macOS 14.4). This context is essential for reproducing UI or performance issues.

## Providing a Good Bug Report

The more detail you provide, the faster Zolt's support team can reproduce and resolve your issue. When reporting a bug, include the following:

- **Steps to reproduce** — A numbered list of exact actions that consistently trigger the issue, starting from the Zolt dashboard or a specific page.
- **Expected behavior** — Describe what you expected to happen when you followed those steps.
- **Actual behavior** — Describe what actually happened instead, including any error messages you saw.
- **Screenshots or screen recording** — Visual evidence is extremely helpful. Annotate screenshots to highlight the relevant area if needed.
- **Browser and OS version** — For example: Firefox 125 on Windows 11, or Safari 17 on macOS Sonoma.
- **Workspace ID** — Found under **Settings > General**. This lets support pull up your account and logs directly.
- **Frequency and consistency** — Note whether the issue happens every time, intermittently, or only under specific conditions (e.g., only with certain file types or on a specific project).
